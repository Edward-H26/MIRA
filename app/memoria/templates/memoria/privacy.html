Privacy

How Memoria handles your data

Your Data

Your conversations, memories, and documents are stored securely and associated with your account. Only you can access your data. Group conversations are shared only with group members.

AI Processing

Messages are processed by AI language models to generate responses. Uploaded documents are processed using OCR to extract text. This data is used solely to provide the service and is not shared with third parties.

Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting the administrator.
