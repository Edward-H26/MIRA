Getting started with Memoria

Interactive Product Tour

Take a guided walkthrough of all Memoria features. The tour will highlight key elements on each page and navigate you through the entire application.

Conversations

Start a conversation by typing a message on the home page or clicking the "+ Chat" button in the sidebar. Your AI agent remembers context within each conversation and learns from your interactions over time. Use @AgentName to mention specific agents and trigger multi-agent collaboration.

Agent Directory

Browse 16 pre-configured AI agents across productivity, marketing, HR, analytics, finance, and legal categories. Click "Message" on any agent card to start a conversation with that agent.

My Agent

Configure your personal AI agent's name, description, and system prompt. Manage auto-generated skills, upload reference documents, and review what your agent has learned in its memory.

Memory

Your agent builds long-term memory from conversations, organized into semantic (facts), episodic (events), and procedural (skills) types. Vote on memories to amplify or suppress them. Memories decay naturally if unused.

Skills Marketplace

Browse and install pre-built skills to enhance your agent's capabilities. Skills like Professional Email Writer, Meeting Summarizer, and Research Analyst give your agent specialized knowledge.

Groups

Create group conversations to collaborate with others. Share an access key to invite members. All group members can interact with agents together.

Dashboard and Analytics

Monitor system performance, user behavior, and cost efficiency across your AI system. Track which models are used (local Qwen vs Gemini API), response latency, and token consumption.

Documents

Upload images or PDFs to extract text using OCR. Scanned documents become reference material that your agent can use for context-aware answers.

Notifications

When agents complete a multi-agent collaboration, you receive a notification. Click the bell icon to see recent notifications with links to the relevant conversations.
