Getting started with Memoria

Conversations

Start a conversation by typing a message on the home page or clicking the "+ Chat" button in the sidebar. Your AI agent remembers context within each conversation and learns from your interactions over time.

Groups

Create group conversations to collaborate with others in real time. Share an access key to invite members. Use the "Group" button in the sidebar or visit the Groups page.

Documents

Upload images of documents to extract text using OCR. Scanned documents are saved to your document hub for future reference. You can also upload files directly in a conversation using the paperclip button.

Memory

Your agent builds a memory of important facts, preferences, and domain knowledge from your conversations. Visit the Memory page to review what your agent has learned.
